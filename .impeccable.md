## Design Context

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** Rigorous · Precise · Calm

**Voice:** Confident and specific, the voice of a researcher who publishes in top venues and lets the work speak. Not
boastful. Technical without being obscure.

**Tone:** Direct, minimal filler. A senior security researcher's site, not a hacker terminal.

**Emotional goals:** Visitors (search committees, tenure reviewers, editors, collaborators, students) should feel
*impressed* and *oriented*: this is serious, high-output cybersecurity research, and it is easy to find what they came
for.

---

### Aesthetic Direction (approved by Ben 2026-10-28, PRs 39 and 40)

**Theme:** Dark, with green as the one accent. The dark background signals the security field; everything else stays
quiet. This replaced the earlier cyberpunk neon direction (glitch, glow, Fira Code headings, `#00ff41`).

**Color palette:**
- Background: `#0a0a0a` to `#0d1117`; card surface `#161b22`
- Text: `#c9d1d9`; headings, section headers (`<summary>` panels), table headers and the site title `#e6edf3` on
  neutral surfaces, never a green tint
- Muted text (venue line `.pub-meta`, secondary info): `#8b949e`
- Accent green: `#4ade80` (10.9:1 on `#0d1117`, 9.9:1 on `#161b22`): links, active nav item, the thin rule under the
  header, small details (tags, metric numbers, disclosure markers, the faint page grid)
- Cyan `#00e5ff`: link hover, icons and the focus ring (`2px solid #00e5ff`)
- Magenta `#e040fb` / `#ff0055`: only inside research charts (tier and trend encodings), never in running text

**Typography:**
- Lato (self-hosted from `static/ccair/assets/fonts/`, preloaded in `layouts/partials/hooks/head-end.html`): body,
  nav, headings (bold) and the site title. No serif.
- Fira Code: only label-like bits (timeline dates, badges, counts, metric numbers, code).
- Merriweather and Playfair Display are still bundled by the theme but are not used; do not reintroduce them.

**Motion and effects:**
- No text glow, glitch effects, uppercase or wide tracking on running text, taglines, job titles, bio or headings.
  Uppercase is fine on small labels and chips.
- At most one or two subtle glows on non-text decoration.
- Faint grid texture on the page background stays.
- Transitions fast (150 to 250 ms) and purposeful; every animation honors `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Anti-references:** Hacker-terminal costume (matrix green text, glitch headings, `///` prompts, "visitor forensics").
Also generic WordPress faculty pages. Playful portfolio sites that undercut scholarly gravitas.

---

### Design Principles

1. **Data is the hero.** Visuals exist to surface research signal (publication quality, topic evolution, citation
   momentum), not to decorate.
2. **Hierarchy over noise.** Accents are sparse; neutral text carries the page. Green marks action and state only.
3. **Restraint.** Calm, plain and specific; nothing that reads as a costume.
4. **Respect the reader's intelligence.** Visitors are researchers; density and terse labels are fine.
5. **WCAG AA.** Readable text 4.5:1, UI parts 3:1. Links in prose keep a faint underline, because the accent green is
   close to the body text in lightness (WCAG 1.4.1). Test labels, tooltips and chart text.

### Technical Constraints

- **Framework:** Hugo + Wowchemy, deployed on Netlify (pins in `netlify.toml`)
- **Shortcodes:** self-contained HTML + `<style>` + `<script>` in `layouts/shortcodes/`
- **Dark/light:** `body.dark` vs `body:not(.dark)`; the day/night toggle is off, so visitors see dark only
- **No backend:** static JSON in `/static/data/` fetched client-side
- **Fonts:** self-hosted only; no third-party font CDNs
- **Charting:** ECharts (`/static/vendor/echarts/`), Leaflet for maps
- **Animations:** CSS keyframes only; no animation library
- **Out of scope for this file:** the CCAIR site, the lab and Pathway Finder (GSU colors and Lato; see `CLAUDE.md`)
