How is the Total Score calculated?

The Altmetric Attention Score is a weighted index that reflects the relative reach of research. Higher weights are assigned to mentions that imply broader societal or economic impact:

  • News: 8 points
  • Policy: 3 points
  • Patents: 3 points
  • Social Media: 1 point

*Mendeley readership counts are tracked to gauge academic uptake but receive a 0 weight in the score calculation.
