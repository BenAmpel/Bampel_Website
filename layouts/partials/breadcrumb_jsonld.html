{{/* BreadcrumbList JSON-LD (schema.org) for every Hugo page except the home page. Home > section(s) > page. The last item is the text of the visible h1: .Title (page_header.html), or the first block title on landing pages. The three publication sections share one parent: the Publications landing page (/publications/), not the bare section list.
