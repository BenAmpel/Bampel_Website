*Last updated October 25, 2026.*

This site is my academic home page (Benjamin M. Ampel, Georgia State University). It also hosts the Center for CyberAI
Research pages under [/ccair/](/ccair/), including the Cyber Career Pathway Finder and the Student Guide. It has no
accounts, no comments, no ads, and nothing is sold. This page lists what is collected and what outside services load.

## Analytics

Pages on bampel.com use **Google Analytics 4** to count page views. Google receives the page address, the page that
linked to it, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (Google Analytics 4
does not store IP addresses). It sets first-party cookies (`_ga`) to tell new visits from returning ones.

- **Google signals and ad personalization are turned off**: the data is used for visit counts, not advertising.
- **Opting out:** if your browser sends [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) or Do Not Track,
  the analytics script does not load at all. Browser extensions that block Google Analytics also work.
- **What I publish:** a monthly job turns the analytics into aggregate counts shown on this site (visitors per month,
  visitors by city, the most-viewed pages, and desktop versus mobile). No individual visit is published.

The Pathway Finder never sends your quiz answers anywhere. They stay in your browser; a results link you copy carries
them after the `#`, which is left out of what analytics records.

## Other services the pages load

| Service | Where | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| [Altmetric](https://www.altmetric.com/privacy-policy/) and [Dimensions](https://www.dimensions.ai/privacy/) | publication pages with attention or citation badges | your IP address and the paper's DOI |
| Esri ArcGIS map tiles | the research map on the home and research pages | your IP address and the map area shown |

Fonts and the other scripts and styles are served from this site, not from a content network.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/), whose servers log requests (IP address, page and
time) to run and protect the service.

## Research studies

The CCAIR Behavioral Lab under [/lab/](/lab/) runs research studies. A study collects data only after you agree to take
part, and its consent form says what it records, how long it keeps it and who can see it. This page does not cover
study data.
