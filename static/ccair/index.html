Center for CyberAI Research (CCAIR)

The Center for CyberAI Research (CCAIR) at Georgia State University advances AI-enabled cybersecurity through three research pillars: detecting social engineering at machine speed (phishing, vishing, and deepfake fraud), understanding hacker communities and the vulnerability lifecycle through large-scale analytics, and building trustworthy AI systems for security operations. The center is directed by Dr. Benjamin M. Ampel (Assistant Professor, Computer Information Systems) and builds on research published in MIS Quarterly, the Journal of Management Information Systems, ACM TMIS, and IEEE ISI.

CCAIR research is supported by federally funded programs including NSF SFS and CICI and an Army Research Lab cooperative agreement, with collaborating researchers at the University of Arizona AI Lab, Indiana University DSAIL, UTSA, and MIT. Graduate researchers work on funded projects spanning phishing URL classification, protected health information leakage in hacker communities, and AI-assisted cybersecurity training.

For students: the Cyber Career Pathway Finder is a free 5-minute quiz and guide to 14 entry-level cybersecurity roles, with the realistic first job, starter certifications and free practice for each.
